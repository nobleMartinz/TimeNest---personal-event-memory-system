About browser notifications

Browser permissions and device settings control whether notifications appear. An ordinary browser tab cannot always wake at an exact future time, so TimeNest checks when the app opens or when you run a check. A future push service can extend this without changing your reminder data.

About local profiles

A profile is stored only on this device and simply puts a password around your own data. TimeNest never sends your email, reminders, or diary entries to a server. If you clear your browser data, the profile and everything it protects are removed together.
